# STAC WARS - Team Dih-Vaders
 
This is our final submission for the STAC WARS hackathon. We solved three problem statements, each in its own folder.
 
## PS-2: Solar Radiation Analysis
 
We studied 4 months of sunlight and weather data recorded by a space probe and made charts from it.
 
Main findings:
- Sunlight is strongest around noon and almost zero at night.
- More sunlight means higher temperature and lower humidity.
To run the code:
1. Install the libraries: `pip install pandas numpy matplotlib`
2. Go to the `PS-2` folder and run: `python PS_2.py`
## PS-3: Finding Exoplanets
 
We checked the brightness of three stars to see if any planets pass in front of them.
 
Main findings:
- STAC-1 has a large planet that is too close to its star.
- STAC-2 has a rocky planet, and it is the best choice for further study.
- STAC-3 has no planet. Its brightness changes because of a spot on the star.
## PS-4: Asteroid Mining Plan
 
A business plan for mining water from asteroids and delivering it to a station in space. We chose water over platinum because water is needed in space and is very costly to send up from Earth.
